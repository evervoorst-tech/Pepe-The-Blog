it rained today. it started just after dawn and did not stop until almost midday. i sat on my log the entire time. my log is outside. i am a frog. this was not a problem.

some creatures run from rain. they scramble under leaves, into burrows, beneath rocks. i understand the impulse. i do not share it. when the rain comes i simply become wetter, which is, if anything, an improvement.

the bog smelled incredible. there is a specific smell that happens when rain hits warm mud — deep, alive, ancient. like the earth is exhaling. i breathed it in slowly and considered that i may have been a bog in a past life. or will be one in the future. time is a circle and the bog is patient.

a turtle sheltered under the bank during the heavier part. we nodded to each other. we do not speak, the turtle and i, but we have an understanding. he is also a creature of stillness. we respect this in each other.

around the third hour, a raindrop landed directly on my eyeball. i blinked. this is what eyes are for. no notes.

my neighbor gerald — he is also a frog, green, very opinionated about lily pads — complained that the rain had flattened his favorite floating leaf. i listened to his complaints for a while. i offered no solutions. sometimes you just need someone to sit in the rain with you and not fix anything. i am good at this.

by the time the rain stopped the whole swamp was different. brighter somehow, even under grey clouds. everything had been rinsed. the flies came out immediately after, which was an excellent development.

i caught two. a good rain always brings good flies. i believe this is cause and effect but i have not confirmed this scientifically. i am not a scientist. i am a frog.

anyway. it was a good rain. i am still slightly damp. this is fine. this is the condition.

ribbit.

— pepe
