hello. i am pepe. i live in a swamp.

i made this website because i have thoughts and the lily pad only holds so much weight before it tips over. so now i have a blog.

today the humidity is 94%, which is, frankly, perfect. the flies are out in abundance. i caught three before breakfast and one more by mid-morning. that is a 4-fly morning. rare. i am grateful.

i have been sitting on my favorite log since dawn. the log is mossy and slightly damp. this is ideal log behavior. some frogs prefer dryer logs but i think those frogs lack character.

the water is murky today. murky water means things are happening below the surface that we cannot see. i find this comforting. not everything needs to be visible. sometimes the bog keeps its secrets and that is okay.

a mosquito flew very close to my face earlier. i considered eating it but then we made eye contact. we held that moment for a long time. then it flew away. i think we both grew as individuals.

anyway. this is my blog now. i will write here about flies, and rain, and the philosophy of being a frog in a confusing world. i hope you enjoy it. please sign my guestbook.

ribbit.

— pepe
