Ribbit.

i have been sitting on my log for six days thinking about flies. not because i am hungry. well. yes because i am hungry. but also because flies are beautiful and i think about them all the time and nobody talks about this enough.
let me tell you about the fly i saw on tuesday.

he was small. he was fast. he did not know i was watching. this is what i like about flies — they are always doing something. buzzing here. buzzing there. landing on a thing. flying off the thing. no plan. no agenda. just vibes. i respect this deeply.

sometimes when the bog is quiet and the water is doing its wet thing, i close my eyes and i think about flies. i imagine a sky that is just flies. wall to wall flies. horizon to horizon. a great fly cloud moving slowly over the pond like a delicious weather system. in this dream i open my mouth and i do not even have to jump. they just come to me. ribbit.
my therapist says i think about flies too much. my therapist is a heron and i do not fully trust him.

the thing people do not understand about flies is the texture. very crunchy on the outside. surprising on the inside. like a tiny present that also has wings. every single time i think — this is the best one. and then the next fly is also the best one. this is called abundance mindset.

i have eaten four flies today. i am going to eat more flies. i am not going to apologise for this.
ribbit.
